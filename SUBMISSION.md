# TinyFish UCL Build Night submission

**Endpoints:** Search, Fetch, Agent

**Build type:** Web app

**What did you build?** Fishies — a pixel-art guide to fish and seafood, with live places to see, eat and buy them.

**Website:** https://fishies-yuvan.vercel.app

**GitHub:** https://github.com/yuvanrajkrishna/fishies

**Tell us about it:**

Pick a fish or sea creature from an illustrated catalogue, explore where it lives on a globe, and learn about its lifecycle. Then find somewhere to see it, eat it, or buy it and cook it, near a location you choose.

TinyFish Search discovers aquarium, restaurant, fishmonger and recipe pages. Fetch reads the live pages and shows species mentions with source links and a check time. Agent investigates a selected venue’s menus or exhibits for more detail, including prices and whether the exact species is confirmed. It stays read-only and makes no bookings or purchases.

The app has 18 creatures, sourced facts, and a consistent retro pixel-art world made with ImageGen: 15 scenes and 138 reusable sprite cells, selected from 70 generated variants. Creatures such as dumbo octopuses get an exploration-only route. A generic menu name is shown as uncertain rather than treated as an exact species match.

Built with Next.js, React and TypeScript. The TinyFish key stays on the server.
